# Key Insights

- Peak hours (7 PM – 10 PM) increase delivery time by ~35%
- Distance strongly impacts delivery duration
- High demand leads to delivery delays

# Recommendations

- Increase delivery partners during peak hours
- Use dynamic allocation strategy
- Introduce incentives during high demand

# A/B Experiment

Control: Normal allocation  
Test: Increased rider allocation  

Metrics:
- Delivery time
- Order success rate
